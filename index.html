Before we work together

A few details.

Do you travel for partnerships?

Yes. I'm based in London and work on UK and international campaigns. Send the destination and proposed dates so we can discuss availability and travel arrangements.

Can you create content for our own channels?

Yes. We can plan vertical videos and edited photography for your channels, alongside or separately from posts to my audience. Usage rights, paid advertising and exclusivity are agreed before the shoot.

What does a partnership include?

The mix depends on the destination and campaign goal. It can include Reels, Stories, photography and destination guides. We agree the deliverables, publishing schedule and review process in the brief.

What should we include in an enquiry?

Your property or destination, proposed dates, campaign goal and budget range. Let me know if you need content for your channels, coverage on mine, or both.

Have a destination or stay in mind? Send me the location, proposed dates and what you'd like the campaign to achieve. I'll get back to you with content ideas and availability.
